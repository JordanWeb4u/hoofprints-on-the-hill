“If everything is okay in this present moment, then everything is okay. If it isn't, embrace it, accept it, and choose to move forward one step at a time.”

Nestled in the tranquil countryside of Culmore, on the border of Donegal in Ireland, lies a place where healing begins with connection. Hoofprints on the Hill is inspired by the real-life characters of Happy Hooves, an equine-assisted therapy charity and sanctuary where humans and animals learn, grow and heal together.

Within this haven, a special and talented little herd — made up of American Miniature Horses, Miniature Shetland Ponies, and a Thoroughbred Ex-Racehorse — bring calm, comfort and joy to all who meet them. Their steady, gentle presence reminds us that even the smallest creatures can make the biggest difference.

Through the lessons and adventures of the whimsical herd, you'll uncover stories of strength, resilience and transformation. You'll learn what it means to face change with courage, to nurture self-belief and acceptance, to find peace in times of grief and worry, and to discover stillness and gratitude in everyday moments — all while getting to know the ponies' unique personalities, full of quirks and charm that might just make you laugh along the way.

The teachings in these stories are intended to stay with you — gentle reminders to help you through life, no matter where you are, no matter your age, and no matter what moment you find yourself in.

Filled with heart, humour and hope, Hoofprints on the Hill reminds you that every emotion has meaning, every mistake brings learning, and every heart — even those most wounded — can heal.

Co-written and created by a BABCP accredited CBT therapist, lecturer, and founder of Happy Hooves, this book draws on evidence-based approaches from Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT). The stories gently teach children practical, evidence-informed strategies to support emotional wellbeing and resilience.
